## S03 Academic Listening format

URL: https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-listening

Access: page_opened; checked 2026-11-02.

Four ten-question parts progress from social dialogue and monologue to educational discussion and academic monologue. Recordings are heard once. The page groups questions into six families and describes word limits. It is not a computer timing implementation specification.

## S04 Academic Reading format

URL: https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-reading

Access: page_opened; checked 2026-11-02.

Three sections and forty questions are completed in sixty minutes. Eleven task families cover selected answers, judgements, matching and completion. More headings than targets are supplied and headings cannot repeat. Completion formats may differ in answer ordering.

## S05 General Training Reading format

URL: https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-general-training-test/ielts-general-training-format-reading

Access: page_opened; checked 2026-11-02.

GT progresses through daily-life texts, workplace texts and a longer general-interest text. The first two sections can contain several texts; three sections do not mean exactly three passages. Its numbered taxonomy differs from the common sample-page introduction.

## S06 Academic Writing format

URL: https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing

Access: search_result; checked 2026-11-02.

Two tasks share sixty minutes. Task 1 reports visual information; Task 2 develops an argument. Minimum lengths differ, and Task 2 receives twice the weight of Task 1. Criteria include task fulfilment, organisation, vocabulary and grammar.

## S07 General Training Writing format

URL: https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-general-training-test/ielts-general-training-format-writing

Access: page_opened; checked 2026-11-02.

Task 1 is a letter based on a situation, three content requirements and an appropriate relationship-dependent register. Task 2 is an essay. The two suggested time allocations fit within one common writing period.

## S08 Speaking format

URL: https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-speaking

Access: page_opened; checked 2026-11-02.

A recorded examiner interview has three parts: familiar questions, an individual long turn with preparation, and a deeper related discussion. The total is eleven to fourteen minutes. Fixed prerecorded prompts are a practice adaptation rather than equivalent interaction.

## S09 IDP computer Listening timing
